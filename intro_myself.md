# Common HR interview questions

1. Why should we hire you?
"I am a fresher, but I have practical experience building projects and working with different technologies. I have a good understanding of programming fundamentals, databases and backend development. I am willing to learn, adapt to new technologies and take responsibility for the work assigned to me. I believe I can contribute to the team while continuously improving my skills."

2. What are your strengths?
"My strengths are problem-solving, willingness to learn and adaptability. I enjoy understanding how things work internally rather than just using tools or technologies. I am also comfortable learning new technologies whenever required."

3. What is your weakness?
"Sometimes I spend more time than necessary trying to understand a problem in depth. I am working on improving my time management and learning when to prioritize practical implementation over excessive research."

4. Why do you want to join our company?
"As a fresher, I am looking for an opportunity to work in a professional environment where I can apply my technical knowledge and gain practical industry experience. I would like to work on real-world projects, learn from experienced developers and grow as a software engineer."

5. Where do you see yourself in five years?
"I see myself as a skilled software engineer with strong technical fundamentals, capable of independently handling development responsibilities and contributing to complex projects. I also want to keep learning and growing with the organization."

6. Are you comfortable learning new technologies?
"Yes, absolutely. I believe technology keeps evolving, so continuous learning is important in software development. I have already worked with multiple technologies across frontend and backend development, and I am comfortable adapting to new tools and frameworks."

7. Are you comfortable working in a team?
"Yes. During my final-year ERP project, I worked in a team of five members. We divided responsibilities across different modules and coordinated our development work. That experience helped me understand teamwork, communication and integration between different modules."

8. Are you willing to relocate to Nagpur?
This is important because the company address is in Nagpur.

If you're comfortable relocating:

"Yes, I am open to relocating to Nagpur for the right opportunity."

If you need to understand the arrangements first:

"I am open to relocation. I would just like to understand the job location, work arrangements and joining requirements before making the necessary arrangements."

# Questions you should ask HR
Question 1: Why is the medical examination fee paid by candidates?

"Could you please explain why the medical examination fee is borne by the candidates and whether this is a one-time mandatory charge?"

Question 2: Will I receive an offer letter before paying?

"Will candidates receive an official offer letter or provisional selection letter mentioning the job role, salary and employment terms before proceeding with the medical examination?"

Question 3: What happens if I don't get selected?

"Since candidates are paying ₹2,450 for the medical examination, could you clarify whether the fee is refundable if a candidate does not clear the final selection process?"

Question 4: Where will the medical examination happen?

"Could you please share the name and address of the hospital or medical centre conducting the examination?"
