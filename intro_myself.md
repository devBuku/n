Here’s a short, professional introduction you can use in a telephonic interview. Keep it natural and conversational rather than memorising every word.

```
Good morning, and thank you for giving me this opportunity.

My name is Shubhayan Bagchi. I am a Computer Science undergraduate from Swami Vivekananda Institute of Science and Technology, affiliated with MAKAUT.

I have a strong interest in software development, particularly full-stack web development. I have hands-on experience working with technologies like JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Node.js, Express.js, and databases like PostgreSQL and MongoDB.

As part of my final-year project, I worked as a backend developer on a College ERP system, where I developed REST APIs for the Library Management module.

I was also a national finalist in Smart India Hackathon 2023 and secured first place in my college tech fest.

I am eager to learn, improve my technical skills, and contribute to real-world projects. That's a brief introduction about me. Thank you.

```


### A few tips for a telephonic interview

1. Keep it under 60–75 seconds. Don't make your introduction too long.
2. Speak slowly and clearly. Smile while speaking; it genuinely helps your tone.
3. Don't list every technology on your resume. Mention only the important ones.
4. Be prepared for follow-up questions about your ERP project, REST APIs, PostgreSQL and your Smart India Hackathon experience.
5. End confidently and let the interviewer take the conversation forward.

My advice: Don't memorise it word for word. Understand the flow and speak naturally. Your introduction should sound like you're talking about yourself, not reading your resume.
