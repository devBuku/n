Notes
Modern Warfare original and reboot are separate timelines.
Black Ops is one continuing universe, not a reboot.
Black Ops Cold War takes place between Black Ops 1 and Black Ops II.
Black Ops 7 follows the future storyline of Black Ops II.
Black Ops III is set much further in the future.
Black Ops 4 has no traditional single-player campaign.
PS4 versions marked ✅ work on PS5 through backward compatibility.
PS3 games marked ❌ cannot be played on PS5.
