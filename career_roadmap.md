# 15. When I Feel Lost or Unmotivated

This is probably the most important section of this note.

Sometimes I will feel like I am not progressing.

I will see people learning Go, Rust, AI, Web3, building impressive projects and getting jobs.

I might feel like I am falling behind.

I might start questioning whether I should learn something else.

When that happens, remember:

- I already have a foundation in full-stack development.
- I have built an ERP system with a team.
- I have explored backend development, networking and real-time communication.
- I don't need another technology to prove that I am learning.
- I need consistency and deeper understanding.
- I need to finish what I have started.
- I need to become comfortable solving problems independently.
- I need to keep applying for opportunities.

**My progress should be measured by what I can build, explain and solve independently — not by how many technologies I know.**

### The 3-month rule

For the next three months, I should focus on:

1. DSA.
2. Computer Science fundamentals.
3. JavaScript and TypeScript.
4. Full-stack / Backend development.
5. Projects.
6. Job applications.

Everything else can wait.

### If I have a bad day

I don't need to complete six hours of studying.

Just do these three things:

- [ ] Solve one DSA problem.
- [ ] Revise one technical concept.
- [ ] Spend 30 minutes on my project.

Then call it a day.

A bad day doesn't have to become a bad week.

### If I don't know what to study today

Open this note and follow this order:

1. Solve DSA problems.
2. Pick one unfinished DBMS / OS / CN topic.
3. Revise one JavaScript or backend concept.
4. Continue the current project.
5. Apply for relevant jobs.

Don't spend an hour deciding what to learn.
